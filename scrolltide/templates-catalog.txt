THE LIBRARY

Animated hero section templates

A hero section is the only part of a landing page every visitor sees. It gets a few seconds to say what the product is and why it is worth reading further, and most of them waste it — a centered headline, a screenshot, a gradient, and nothing that would look out of place on a competitor's site.

Every template here is a scroll-driven hero built around motion rather than a static image, for a specific kind of product: analytics platforms, focus apps, AI builders, security tools, design studios. Each comes with the prompt that generates it, so you can paste it into Claude, Cursor or v0 and get working code rather than a screenshot to copy by hand.

156 templates in the library, 8 of them free.
